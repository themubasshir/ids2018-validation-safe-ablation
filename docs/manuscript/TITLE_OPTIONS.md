# Pass 1 title finalists

No title is final in Pass 1. These finalists come directly from the Stage29 title registry and preserve the validation-framework framing.

## 1. Most conservative

**A Multi-Axis Validation Audit of Machine-Learning Intrusion Detection**

This title states the empirical object and scope without implying a new model, universal failure, or deployment proof. It is the safest option if the target venue favors conventional descriptive titles.

## 2. Strongest journal title

**Beyond Benchmark Scores: A Validation Framework for Intrusion Detection**

This is the strongest synthesis of the manuscript's central inference problem and framework contribution. “Beyond” signals added validity checks without dismissing benchmark evaluation, while “framework” accurately describes the connected empirical audit.

## 3. Most memorable while defensible

**What Survives the Benchmark? A Multi-Axis Audit of Intrusion Detection**

The question mirrors the conclusion the paper is designed to answer. It remains defensible because the manuscript preserves both surviving capabilities and failed or conditional inferences rather than asserting uniform collapse.

## Deferred decision

Final selection should occur only after Pass 2 reference verification, venue targeting, and review of the integrated manuscript's emphasis. Sensational or universal-negative titles remain prohibited.
