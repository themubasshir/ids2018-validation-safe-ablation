## Detailed finalists

### 1. Computers & Security
Best specialist fit. Official scope explicitly covers security research and applications; recent 2025 content includes SAFE-IDS and other IDS/security application studies. The paper's protocol audit, conditional findings, and reproducibility boundaries fit. Its explicit exclusion concerns security of AI/ML systems themselves, not ML used for IDS evaluation. Subscription avoids mandatory publication charge. Requires Elsevier declarations, originality, funding, competing interests, data/code statement, permissions, and AI-use disclosure.

### 2. ACM TOPS
Strongest explicit methodological/security-evaluation match: practical relevance to construction, evaluation, application, or operation of security-critical systems; intrusion detection is listed. Author guidelines impose 35 total ACM-style pages and require a strong original contribution, not a survey/tutorial. ACM became fully OA in 2026; temporary APC pricing and ACM Open eligibility must be rechecked. Artifact/reproducibility policy is compatible with the provenance layer. Recent issues demonstrate empirical security/privacy systems research, though not necessarily IDS protocol audits.

### 3. JISA
Best balanced target. Scope explicitly covers original research and practice-driven information-security applications. Recent 2025/2026 issues include intrusion-detection, adversarial robustness, and security analytics articles. Fit is stronger than broad computing venues. Guide requires abstract <=250 words, citation/reference consistency, declarations, and supplementary-file compliance. Subscription route preserves a no-mandatory-APC path.

### 4. IEEE TDSC
Methodological fit through dependable/security evaluation and performance tradeoffs. Recent issues include secure systems and AI/security work. The paper must emphasize archival evaluation methodology and system dependability, not only benchmark criticism. IEEE author template, anonymization/review rules, page/overlength charges, and OA price require current title-specific confirmation.

### 5. IEEE TIFS
Recognized security venue; network intrusion detection and adversarial ML appear within the broad scope. Recent content is strongly forensics, signal processing, biometrics, and security analytics. A pure IDS evaluation audit without signal-processing/forensics novelty is a material fit risk. Hybrid; official page gives USD 2,800 OA APC.

### 6. JNCA
Strong network application and IDS fit; recent issues include intrusion detection and explainable network-security research. Requires stronger network-application/deployment narrative than the current audit-only framing. Hybrid subscription route; supplementary files must be submitted, cited, and captioned.

### 7. IJIS
Direct information-security scope includes network security, applications, and implementations. Fully OA from January 2026, with official APC and discretionary waiver route. Recent content includes intrusion-detection explainability and security operations. Strong fallback if OA funding exists; cost is the main drawback.

### 8. Cybersecurity
Scope explicitly includes cybersecurity data analytics and data-driven security/measurement studies, making the scientific fit strong. Recent content should be checked at submission for comparable empirical evaluation. Fully OA pricing and indexing/ranking require current confirmation. Good balanced target if APC support exists.

### 9. IEEE Access
Explicitly accepts applied research with quantitative validation and has a negative-results/debate structure. No page limit; Word and PDF required; minimum 3 and maximum 10 keywords; ORCID and biographies required; AI-generated text disclosure required. Strong practical fallback, but reputation and broad-scope selectivity are weaker than specialist venues.

### 10. The Computer Journal
Legitimate broad-computing venue, but security evaluation is not its central identity. Use only if the manuscript is reframed as a general empirical computing-methodology contribution. Official OUP LaTeX/BST and licensing guidance available; current length and supplement details require portal confirmation.
