## Exclusions and warnings

- **Computers & Security — excluded for this manuscript.** Elsevier's current aims/guide states that, since early 2024, it has a moratorium on submissions featuring AI/ML as significant components, including applying AI/ML to system security/privacy. This manuscript audits ML intrusion detection; no applicable exception is stated. Its excellent reputation and SJR 2025 Q1 do not override the explicit scope barrier.
- **Journal of Network and Systems Management — excluded unless reframed.** The official scope says manuscripts must cover network-management issues and solutions; this manuscript is an evaluation audit, not a management solution.
- **Journal of Cybersecurity — conditional, not preferred.** OUP requires interdisciplinary approach, broad societal implications, or substantial human/organizational challenge. The current technical audit does not clearly satisfy that threshold.
- **IEEE Transactions on Networking, Pattern Recognition Letters, Information Sciences, Computers & Electrical Engineering, Ad Hoc Networks — weak or conditional fit.** Core protocol, method, electrical-system, or deployment novelty is not the manuscript contribution.
- **Security and Communication Networks — hold.** Current indexing, publisher policy, and APC evidence not independently verified in this assessment.
- **Journal of Computer Security — hold.** Current coverage and publishing status require direct IOS Press confirmation before use.
