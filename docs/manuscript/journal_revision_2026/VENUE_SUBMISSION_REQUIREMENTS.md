## Manuscript-specific package

1. Editable JISA-formatted manuscript.
2. Abstract reduced to <=250 words.
3. One to seven keywords.
4. Separate highlights file.
5. Six figures and six tables, cited and captioned.
6. S1–S7 supplementary files, each named, cited, and bounded by the manuscript's reproducibility restrictions.
7. Cover letter emphasizing empirical security evaluation, not a new IDS architecture.
8. Author metadata, affiliations, corresponding-author email, ORCID if requested by portal.
9. Funding statement; conflict-of-interest statement; author-contribution statement.
10. Data/code availability statement preserving closed-data, historical-target, static-verification, and hardware-boundary restrictions.
11. Prior-publication/overlap statement covering repository and any qualifying conference version.
12. AI-assisted-writing/artwork disclosure if applicable under the live guide.

## Cost and access

JISA is presented by Elsevier as supporting open access and has a subscription route. No mandatory fee is established for the subscription route in the accessible materials. Current OA APC, taxes, waiver eligibility, institutional agreements, and any page/color charges are **UNVERIFIED** here; obtain the live quote after article type and corresponding-author country are entered.

## Author eligibility

The portal requests a corresponding author and contact email. No evidence requires current university employment, supervisor status, IEEE membership, or publisher subscription for basic submission. The corresponding author must accurately identify affiliations and work location. Authorship must follow Elsevier/COPE standards; do not add or omit a contributor to satisfy affiliation or APC conditions.

## Pre-upload gate

- Confirm live JISA guide and portal fields.
- Confirm current indexing, APC, waiver, and subscription terms.
- Confirm article type and any word/length rule not exposed in the accessible guide.
- Validate all citations/references.
- Confirm figure resolution/file formats and supplementary upload limits.
- Complete declarations and AI disclosure.
- Obtain final author approval.
- Submit only after author review; no submission has occurred.

## Uncertainties

JCR quartile, SCIE/ESCI status, current APC/waiver amount, article-length limit, exact figure specifications, portal-specific file limits, ORCID enforcement, and final article-type labels require live portal confirmation.
