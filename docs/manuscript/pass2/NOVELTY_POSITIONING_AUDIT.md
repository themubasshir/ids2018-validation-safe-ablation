# Pass 2A Novelty Positioning Audit

Status: verified against the selected Pass 2A literature set through 2026-08-23. This is a positioning audit, not a systematic-review or priority claim.

## Decision rule

The search found substantial prior work on closed-world limitations, temporal and prevalence bias, dataset artifacts, cross-dataset transfer, open-set evaluation, deployment measurement, and reproducibility. None of the six manuscript contributions may therefore imply invention of those component ideas. A contribution remains distinct when it describes what this study implements, evaluates, connects, or preserves across its frozen evidence chain without asserting field-wide priority.

## Priority-language audit

- No occurrence of “first ever” or “first comprehensive” is permitted.
- “First framework” is not supported by this bounded search.
- “Comprehensive” would require a separate systematic-review design and is not used as a novelty claim.
- Preferred verbs are “we apply,” “we evaluate,” “we connect,” and “we preserve.”

## Outcome

Five contribution statements are defensible as distinct study-specific contributions. Contribution 1 requires one narrow wording change so that prior evaluation frameworks and component methods are not implicitly displaced. No contribution is classified `OVERLAPS_PRIOR_WORK` in the sense of duplicating the same frozen empirical chain, and no contribution retains a `REFERENCE_GAP_REMAINS` status after the documented narrowing.
