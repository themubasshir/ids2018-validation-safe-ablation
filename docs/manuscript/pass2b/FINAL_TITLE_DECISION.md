# Final Title Decision

Status: **FROZEN for the Pass 2B submission candidate**  
Decision basis: `docs/manuscript/TITLE_OPTIONS.md`, the Pass 2A novelty-positioning audit, and the six Stage29-governed contributions.

## Recommended final title

**Beyond Benchmark Scores: A Multi-Axis Validation Framework for Intrusion Detection**

This title centers evaluation rather than a new detector, preserves the manuscript's connected-framework contribution, and signals that benchmark evidence is retained but insufficient for broader claims. “Multi-axis” distinguishes the study-specific integration without implying invention of temporal, cross-dataset, open-set, operational, or reproducibility evaluation. It makes no universal failure, priority, architecture, or deployment claim.

## Conservative alternative

**A Multi-Axis Validation Audit of Machine-Learning Intrusion Detection**

This is the safest descriptive option for a venue that favors conventional titles. “Audit” accurately bounds the work to the evaluated datasets, learners, bridges, operating rules, and hardware.

## Memorable alternative

**What Survives the Benchmark? A Multi-Axis Audit of Intrusion Detection**

The question captures the paper's evidence-preserving structure: some capabilities survive, while others remain conditional, directional, learner-dependent, or unsupported. It avoids implying uniform collapse.

## Stability rule

The recommended title is used in `manuscript/manuscript_submission_candidate.md`. A later change requires a dated rationale tied to venue scope or verified editorial consistency; scientific results alone may not be reframed through a title change.
