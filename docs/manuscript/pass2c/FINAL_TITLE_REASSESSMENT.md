# Final Title Reassessment

Status: **FROZEN for the Pass 2C candidate**

## Options considered

### A. A Multi-Axis Validation Framework for Machine-Learning Intrusion Detection

This is the recommended title. The empirical object is specifically machine-learning intrusion detection: the paper evaluates fitted tree, ensemble, and neural paths, their scores and operating points, and their validation behavior. Retaining “Machine-Learning” prevents the framework from appearing to cover signature-based or other intrusion-detection systems not evaluated here. The title is concise, centers the framework, and makes no result-specific, architectural, priority, or universal-failure claim.

### B. A Multi-Axis Validation Framework for Intrusion Detection

This is shorter but broader than the evidence. “Intrusion Detection” alone can be read as applying equally to non-machine-learning detection systems. The manuscript's recommendations may be informative beyond the evaluated learners, but Pass 2C cannot enlarge the empirical scope through the title.

### C. Beyond Benchmark Scores: A Multi-Axis Validation Framework for Intrusion Detection

The Pass 2B title accurately framed the benchmark-versus-capability distinction. Its opening phrase is memorable, but it is less direct and retains the broader “Intrusion Detection” object. The Pass 2C title states the paper type and evaluated domain more precisely.

## Decision

Use **A Multi-Axis Validation Framework for Machine-Learning Intrusion Detection**.

The result-specific alternative “Development Regime, Not Architecture, Determines Forward Transfer” is rejected because the study did not establish that causal or architecture-irrelevance claim and because the paper integrates eight validity checks rather than one temporal finding.
