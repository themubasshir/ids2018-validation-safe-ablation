# Scientific execution boundary

## Final empirical wall

Stage28 is the final empirical stage in this repository's accepted scientific
lineage. Its terminal authority is the frozen closure artifact:

`results/stage28_stability_novelty_control/stage28_final_synthesis/stage28_final_synthesis_receipt.json`

That receipt records 108 authorized and 108 consumed new fits, 12 historical
reuses, zero remaining fits, 50 chronology-LOAO seed realizations, 50
random-LOAO seed realizations and ten shared-final-holdout ensemble
evaluations. It also records `stage29_authorized: false` and limits the next
work to manuscript integration.

## Repository work after Stage28

Stage29 and all later work in this repository are non-experimental unless a
separate future research project receives explicit scientific authorization.
Reproducibility engineering, manuscript integration, documentation, archival
source recovery, configuration extraction, byte hashing and static tests do
not authorize scientific recomputation.

The current safe interfaces may:

- describe frozen methodology with `--dry-run`;
- verify declared paths, sizes and SHA256 identities with `--verify-only`;
- parse small frozen JSON/CSV metadata for static invariants;
- evaluate Stage25's published analytic formulas from already-frozen scalar
  inputs; and
- exercise generic timing-statistic helpers on toy arrays that are not
  historical observations.

They may not:

- fit or refit any model;
- run model inference;
- open or rematerialize a target or holdout;
- deserialize Stage28 probability arrays for scientific computation;
- regenerate probabilities, bootstraps, prevalence tables, timing samples,
  memory profiles, statistics or figures;
- select or reselect a threshold, model, feature set, seed, split or
  qualitative cutoff;
- modify or replace a frozen artifact; or
- begin a Stage29 experiment or reinterpret Stage28 as permission for one.

## Future experiments

Any future experiment belongs to a separate research project with its own
prospective protocol, data-access authority, fit and opening budgets, result
namespace, branch and review boundary. It must not be presented as a
continuation implicitly authorized by these Stage25-28 reproducibility files.
