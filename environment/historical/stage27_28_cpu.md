3.2.0, LightGBM 4.6.0 and Joblib 1.5.3 with CPU-only execution.

1.6.1. Its XGBoost and LightGBM versions remain `VERSION_NOT_PROVEN`; Stage27
versions must not be inherited. Stage28 is the final empirical wall.
