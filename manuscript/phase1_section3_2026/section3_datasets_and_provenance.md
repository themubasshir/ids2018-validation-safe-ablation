# 3. Datasets and Provenance

This study uses several evaluation populations derived from two related benchmark families, but they are not interchangeable. The conventional CSE-CIC-IDS2018 reference, source-faithful temporal populations, CICIDS2017 transfer populations, known-category slices, and leave-one-attack-family-out (LOAO) targets have different construction rules, prevalence, provenance, and inferential roles. Results are therefore reported against the population that generated them rather than pooled across populations.

## 3.1 CSE-CIC-IDS2018 Reference Dataset and Processing

CSE-CIC-IDS2018 is the principal benchmark lineage. The early conventional evaluation used an externally supplied processed binary table with 300,928 rows and 78 numeric predictors after excluding the original `Label` field and the binary target. The frozen reference memberships were stratified into training, validation, and holdout partitions containing 192,593, 48,149, and 60,186 rows, respectively. Their class counts were 115,200 benign and 77,393 attack rows in training; 28,800 benign and 19,349 attack rows in validation; and 36,000 benign and 24,186 attack rows in the holdout. The partition ratios were 64%, 16%, and 20%.

The reference roles were deliberately narrow. Training supported model fitting and, for historically scaled learners, fitting the scaler. Validation supported model and operating-point selection. The holdout was reserved for the frozen reference evaluation. The Stage01 protocol specifies training-only `StandardScaler` fitting for the scaled learners; the tree-boosting path retained its recorded preprocessing. The reference table is processed and stratified rather than a natural-prevalence forward population. Its membership receipts establish disjoint reference roles, not session independence, temporal ordering, or deployment representativeness.

The reference corpus also differs from the later source-faithful temporal populations in representation and provenance. The historical processed input is external to the repository, and the original reference split was not recomputed during this manuscript phase. Accordingly, reference counts and results are treated as frozen evidence, with partial static reproducibility rather than as a newly regenerated dataset. The reference population supports within-dataset benchmark claims only; it is not used as evidence for temporal transfer, cross-dataset portability, prevalence behavior, or unseen-family detection.

## 3.2 Source-Faithful Temporal Evaluation Populations

Temporal evaluation uses a distinct source lineage based on dated CSE-CIC-IDS2018 daily exports. The lineage retains chronological source-day information and a frozen ordered 70-feature model-input contract for the later temporal studies. Timestamp provenance was recovered at one-second resolution for 192,521 authorized non-holdout rows. The recovery established unique temporal provenance for 85.36783% of those rows; 15.55201% of temporal bins contained mixed train/validation membership, and 99.76558% of validation bins were within 30 seconds of a training bin in the Stage18 audit. This audit showed that the inherited earlier membership was unsuitable for temporal-window claims. It did not invalidate every tabular use of that membership, and it did not establish concept drift.

The later Stage22R temporal populations apply the frozen K79 exact-identity control to the historical Kaggle daily lineage. K79 consists of the corrected integer-second timestamp plus the 78 common numeric predictors, excluding the label. Predictor values are canonicalized as IEEE-754 float64 values; NaN is canonicalized, positive and negative infinity remain distinct, and negative zero is normalized to positive zero. A BLAKE2b-128 digest accelerates candidate matching, while exact canonical-record comparison verifies candidate collisions.

Development conflicts and exact duplicates were removed before split construction. Same-label duplicates retained one deterministic representative, while mixed-label signatures were excluded. At the single authorized opening of the Mar1--Mar2 forward target, retained development signatures had priority; the frozen K79 rule then excluded matching target signatures, removed within-target mixed-label conflicts, and collapsed same-label target duplicates deterministically. Before this cleaning, the two target days contained 1,379,675 effective rows. Five mixed-label signatures accounted for 10 excluded rows, and same-label duplicate collapse removed 5,532 rows. The resulting common forward population contained 1,374,133 rows: 998,788 benign and 375,345 attack rows, with attack prevalence 0.2731504155711274.

The K79 result establishes exact timestamp-plus-predictor identity separation between retained development and the shared forward target under the frozen rule. It does **not** establish S4 independence, session independence, endpoint or source-IP independence, or five-tuple independence, because endpoint fields were not uniformly retained across the historical daily files. These distinctions are material: K79 controls exact available-record identity, not latent traffic-session or infrastructure relationships.

The temporal development design crossed split geometry with training prevalence: random versus chronological development and natural versus training-only rebalanced training data. The random split used a stratified partition of the K79-clean development universe. The chronological split trained on earlier source days and validated on the later development day. Rebalancing was confined to training; validation and the forward target were not resampled. All four frozen development cells used the same retained feature contract and the Stage22R equal-weight LightGBM/XGBoost ensemble. Development thresholds and model scope were fixed before the shared forward target was opened. The forward target was historically opened once and later reused for registered stability analysis; it is therefore not presented as a newly blind holdout.

## 3.3 CICIDS2017 and Cross-Dataset Transfer Populations

CICIDS2017 provides the second benchmark domain. The transfer analysis preserves its separate source identity, daily chronology, preprocessing, and target roles rather than treating it as another partition of IDS2018. The primary IDS2018-to-CICIDS2017 direction evaluated 2,830,743 effective target rows, comprising 557,646 attack and 2,273,097 benign rows. The reciprocal CICIDS2017-to-IDS2018 direction evaluated the frozen IDS2018 February 28 target, containing 593,780 rows: 62,256 attack and 531,524 benign, with attack prevalence 0.10484691299808009. These direction-specific populations were not averaged because source learners, target roles, preprocessing, and prevalence structures differ.

Cross-dataset comparability was governed by frozen semantic bridges. Bridge62 contains 62 shared predictors and excludes the eight aggregate flag-count fields: `FIN Flag Cnt`, `SYN Flag Cnt`, `RST Flag Cnt`, `PSH Flag Cnt`, `ACK Flag Cnt`, `URG Flag Cnt`, `CWE Flag Count`, and `ECE Flag Cnt`. Bridge70 retains the exact 70-feature semantic mapping and keeps published and flag-corrected serializations distinct. No fuzzy mapping, target-label-guided mapping, or target-value-guided mapping was used. The primary bridge62 published representation and its flag-corrected counterpart were bitwise identical because the corrected aggregate fields were excluded; bridge70 was retained as a preregistered representation-sensitivity audit.

Source training, validation thresholds, feature contracts, cleaning, and target roles were frozen independently for each transfer direction. Target labels did not guide fitting, feature selection, calibration, thresholding, or bridge construction. The two planned `GROUNDED_S4` cells were cancelled before target opening because durable physical-row membership could not be reconstructed without a new heuristic. No fuzzy substitute, inferred value, slot reallocation, or replacement target was used. The cancellation is a provenance and eligibility outcome, not a negative model result.

The transfer populations are consequently source- and bridge-specific. They support directional conclusions for the evaluated IDS2018/CICIDS2017 contracts only. They do not establish portability to arbitrary datasets, symmetry between the two directions, or independence from all semantic and preprocessing differences.

## 3.4 Attack-Family Taxonomy and Evaluation Eligibility

Attack labels were represented through a frozen seven-family taxonomy: `BOT`, `DDOS`, `DOS`, `AUTH_BRUTE_FORCE`, `INFILTRATION`, `PORT_SCAN`, and `WEB_ATTACK`. The earlier processed-reference category audit and the later LOAO analysis serve different purposes. Known-category slices in the processed reference holdout describe error concentration among categories present in development; they are not withheld-family tests. For example, the Stage11 known-category `Infilteration` support was 3,967, whereas the Stage27 LOAO `Infiltration` target had only 36 held-out positives. These are distinct populations, not conflicting counts.

LOAO eligibility required zero exposure of the held-out family in both training and validation, day-atomic chronology satisfying `TRAIN < VALIDATION < TARGET`, and sufficient target support for the intended inference. Five families had eligible fold receipts: BOT, DDOS, INFILTRATION, PORT_SCAN, and WEB_ATTACK. `DOS` and `AUTH_BRUTE_FORCE` were structurally ineligible under the frozen chronology and were not converted into model failures. `INFILTRATION` remained descriptive because its held-out target support was 36 positives, below the preregistered minimum of 50 for inferential family claims. Inferential interpretation was therefore limited to eligible, sufficiently supported family-learner cells.

The primary family target paired the withheld family with temporally matched benign traffic. A secondary context population containing known attacks was retained separately. XGBoost and LightGBM were evaluated independently. Thresholds were selected from known-family validation only; held-out targets did not drive threshold search or post-target changes. Ranking metrics and frozen-threshold operating metrics were retained as separate quantities, and no pooled novelty or universal zero-day score was formed. LOAO therefore measures behavior on eligible benchmark families under a defined withholding protocol, not detection of arbitrary future attacks.

## 3.5 Data Provenance, Integrity, and Experimental Governance

The repository distinguishes provenance states rather than treating every artifact as raw data. `Raw-exact` denotes preserved source bytes; `published` denotes released artifact semantics; `corrected` denotes a frozen documented correction; `source-faithful` denotes a derived representation following authenticated source semantics and locked cleaning; and `reconstructed` denotes recovery from surviving lineage evidence without a claim of byte identity. These states remain attached to populations, manifests, and result artifacts.

Each stage has a protocol configuration, canonical source record, result or evidence registry, and—where available—size and SHA-256 receipts. The Stage01 reference protocol identifies the external processed input, feature contract, split ratios, and training-only scaler rule. The Stage18, Stage22, Stage24, Stage27, and Stage28 protocol locks identify their historical notebooks or scripts, frozen inputs, target-opening rules, fit budgets, and verification artifacts. Large models, arrays, closed targets, and external source data may be hash-verified without being deserialized or reopened.

Development data were limited to their declared roles: fitting, validation selection, and protocol checks. Target-facing model scope, feature mappings, cleaning rules, thresholds, and family eligibility were frozen before the corresponding target evaluation. No post-target fitting, feature search, calibration, threshold reselection, bridge remapping, or replacement of cancelled cells was permitted. Stage28 is the final empirical wall: its closure records 108 of 108 authorized new fits, 12 declared artifact reuses, and zero remaining planned cells. Stage29 synthesis introduced no scientific computation.

The governance records also preserve negative and incomplete outcomes. Unsupported backends remain unsupported; cancelled target cells remain cancelled; Stage18 ViT feasibility was a representation-feasibility rejection rather than a performance result; and Stage18 graph and Stage21 CNN/ViT findings retain descriptive or supplement-only status. These records prevent manuscript prose from converting unavailable evidence into a result.

## 3.6 Data Availability and Reproducibility Boundaries

The repository provides protocol JSON files, safe stage interfaces, extracted or archival implementations, archived notebooks, result manifests, frozen summaries, evidence registries, claim registries, source maps, and reproducibility audits. The canonical traceability path is manuscript claim, frozen result or exhibit, stage configuration and safe interface, implementation or preserved notebook cell, then provenance and equivalence record. The repository-wide audit reports configuration and safe-entry-point coverage for Stages 01--28, with heterogeneous equivalence levels that describe the verified claim rather than guarantee an end-to-end rerun.

The original IDS2018 and CICIDS2017 source data, several processed corpora, some historical models and probability or explanation arrays, and parts of the historical software environments are external, unavailable, or intentionally closed. Closed targets and holdouts are not reopened by validation tooling. Early stages consequently remain partially reproducible static scientific evidence. Stage25 supports deterministic scalar reproduction from frozen inputs; Stage26 preserves methodology and archival hardware timings but not portable timing constants; the remaining stages retain their declared partial or static boundaries. In particular, hash verification of a model or array is not equivalent to loading it and reproducing its scientific output.

The supported reproduction level is therefore claim- and artifact-level verification, plus exact deterministic checks where explicitly classified. A complete local regeneration of every historical metric, model, explanation, timing, and target result is not claimed. The supplementary material should cross-reference the stage-specific reproducibility matrix, source registries, notebook maps, hashes, cancelled-cell records, and the full limitation matrix so that readers can distinguish available code and receipts from unavailable inputs and closed scientific targets.
