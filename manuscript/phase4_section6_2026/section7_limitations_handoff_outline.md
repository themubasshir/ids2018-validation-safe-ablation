# Section 7 Limitations Handoff Outline

Section 7 is not drafted here. This outline separates study limitations from the interpretive discussion.

## 7.1 Population and external-validity limits

- Processed, stratified reference population is not natural-prevalence, temporal, or deployment evidence.
- Temporal and transfer populations remain benchmark-derived and direction-specific.
- No arbitrary-enterprise, live-network, or current-threat generalization claim.

## 7.2 Temporal and identity limits

- Chronology and attack-family composition are entangled.
- K79 controls exact corrected-second-plus-predictor identity only.
- No S4, session, endpoint, source-IP, or five-tuple independence claim.
- Stage28 reused the historically opened target; it is not a new blind holdout.

## 7.3 Transfer and cancellation limits

- Bridge62 and bridge70 represent distinct semantic contracts.
- Serialization sensitivity is conditional on frozen target and arrays.
- Two `GROUNDED_S4` cells were cancelled before target opening.

## 7.4 Operational translation limits

- Stage25 assumes fixed TPR/FPR under prior shift.
- Traffic, analyst service time, capacity, and relative costs are scenarios.
- No observed SOC workflow, production alert correlation, or organization-specific economics.

## 7.5 Computational limits

- Stage26 measures prepared-input-to-probability components only.
- Hardware, software, backend, batch, and resource conditions restrict portability.
- Unsupported paths have no timing estimate.
- No end-to-end latency, energy, or operational throughput claim.

## 7.6 Unseen-family limits

- DOS and AUTH_BRUTE_FORCE are ineligible.
- Infiltration has 36 held-out positives and remains descriptive.
- LOAO family behavior does not establish arbitrary zero-day detection.
- Known-category `Infilteration` support 3967 is a different population.

## 7.7 Reproducibility and evidence limits

- External raw datasets, closed targets, historical models/arrays, and environments are unavailable or restricted.
- Stage25 supports deterministic scalar reproduction; Stage26 preserves method and archival timings.
- Other stages remain partial/static or hash-verifiable.
- Phase 4 introduces no new empirical computation.

## 7.8 Methodological traceability limits

- Phase 2's 15 records cover major method families, but not every operation at one-record-per-operation granularity.
- Threshold grid/tie-breaking, exact Stage25 equations, Stage26 environment details, Stage27 eligibility gates, and Stage28 control linkage require cross-reading protocols and source artifacts.
- Phase 3's 22-record result matrix covers material numerical claims, not exhaustive implementation provenance.
