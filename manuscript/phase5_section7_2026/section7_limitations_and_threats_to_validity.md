# 7. Limitations and Threats to Validity

The audit establishes several bounded findings: strong discrimination under the processed reference condition, higher chronological than random forward ranking under the registered temporal protocol, directional cross-dataset transfer, selective eligible-family behavior, and computational feasibility for measured inference paths. These findings are not interchangeable. The limitations below define the populations, controls, uncertainty objects, and evidence states that constrain their interpretation. They are organized as threats to internal, construct, external, statistical-conclusion, and reproducibility validity where that classification clarifies the relevant boundary; the classification is editorial, not a new empirical result.

## 7.1 Dataset and Benchmark Scope

The empirical chain is centered on CSE-CIC-IDS2018 and a restricted CICIDS2017 bridge rather than live operational networks. This limits external validity to the evaluated source lineages, cleaning rules, representations, and target roles. The processed reference remains useful as a controlled benchmark condition, but its stratified, rebalanced construction does not represent natural deployment prevalence, forward chronology, enterprise traffic, or current attack activity. Accordingly, its strong F1, ROC-AUC, and PR-AUC establish within-reference discrimination; they do not establish performance in an arbitrary enterprise, cloud, IoT, or operational network.

The temporal and transfer populations reduce some of this uncertainty without removing it. They preserve source-day or source-dataset structure, but remain benchmark-derived populations with frozen contracts. Cross-dataset conclusions depend on direction-specific source learners, preprocessing, prevalence, and the bridge62 or bridge70 semantic mapping. The two directions therefore cannot be treated as a general portability test. Bridge62 excludes the eight aggregate flag-count fields, while bridge70 retains published and corrected serialization as separate conditions. The measured bridge sensitivity is consequently a threat to construct validity of any claim that treats a shared column name or a larger feature intersection as a complete semantic equivalence.

Two planned `GROUNDED_S4` cells were cancelled before target opening because durable physical-row membership could not be reconstructed without a new heuristic. No fuzzy substitute, slot reassignment, or replacement target was used. This preserves internal validity for the completed cells, but leaves the intended sensitivity analysis incomplete. The cancelled cells provide no negative model evidence and cannot support a claim that all preregistered transfer conditions were evaluated.

Some historical branches are narrower still. The Stage18 graph result is restricted to an endpoint-authenticated February 20 source, so unseen topology was not estimable. The Stage21 CNN/ViT comparison is a single descriptive frozen comparison rather than a confirmatory architecture study. These results remain useful as representation and architecture diagnostics, but they cannot establish graph deployment readiness, unseen-host generalization, or universal ViT/CNN superiority. External raw source material and some processed artifacts are also unavailable; provenance and frozen summaries mitigate this evidence-availability threat without making the populations fully observable or representative.

## 7.2 Temporal, Protocol, and Attack-Family Validity

The principal temporal threat is confounding between chronology and attack-family composition. The chronological and random development geometries do not differ only in ordering; their family composition and other population properties also differ. The higher chronological forward ranking observed under the frozen protocol therefore cannot be assigned uniquely to chronology, concept drift, or any other mechanism. Stage28's matched random-LOAO and family-aware controls partially constrain this interpretation, but their mixed, family-specific outcomes did not identify a unique cause.

The identity control is deliberately narrower than a general independence guarantee. K79 uses the corrected integer-second timestamp and the 78 common numeric predictors, with exact canonical-record verification after candidate matching. It controls exact available-record identity under the frozen cleaning rule. It does not establish session, endpoint, source-IP, five-tuple, or S4 independence. Latent relationships among flows, infrastructure, and collection events therefore remain possible and constrain claims about leakage or independence.

Stage28 also reused the historically opened Stage22 shared forward target. Its five-seed analysis is a robustness re-evaluation under registered conditions, not an independent blind external holdout or prospective replication. This restriction affects both internal and statistical-conclusion validity: repeated frozen analyses can assess realization sensitivity, but cannot substitute for a newly sampled target population.

LOAO scope is constrained by eligibility. DOS and AUTH_BRUTE_FORCE were structurally ineligible under the day-atomic chronology and were not failed detection experiments. Five families were eligible, but support was heterogeneous. Infiltration had 36 held-out positives and therefore remains descriptive. This must be kept separate from the Stage11 known-category `Infilteration` support of 3,967 in the processed reference audit; the counts describe different populations and inferential roles. LOAO consequently supports selective family-specific behavior, not universal unseen-family or zero-day detection.

## 7.3 Statistical and Model-Realization Uncertainty

Several comparisons use historical paired bootstrap intervals calculated on one frozen evaluation population, with procedures specific to the relevant stage. These intervals are conditional on the retained samples or arrays, implementation, and protocol. They describe uncertainty for the declared comparison; they do not provide population-wide guarantees or convert a benchmark contrast into an unconditional significance claim. A confidence interval containing zero leaves the learner difference unresolved; it does not prove equivalence. Conversely, an interval excluding zero does not expand the target population or eliminate the remaining construct and external-validity limits.

The finite seed program provides a complementary uncertainty object. Stage28 used five registered seeds, fixed recipes, no best-seed selection, and no full hyperparameter search at every seed. The chronological-over-random ordering held in five of five realizations, but that count is descriptive stability over the registered realizations, not a population-level probability, corrected significance test, or independent dataset replication. Seed variation was not combined with bootstrap uncertainty, and no new significance calculation is introduced here.

The model-realization scope also differs across stages. Some reference, temporal, transfer, and LOAO results rely on frozen learner fits or inherited operating points, while the stability program covers only designated cells. This supports the reported claim ceilings but limits generalization to other seeds, tuning procedures, learners, feature realizations, and future targets. The controls and fixed recipes mitigate selective reporting, yet they do not remove finite-sample, model-selection, or population-sampling uncertainty.

## 7.4 Operational and Deployment-Assumption Validity

Stage25 is an analytic prior-probability-shift translation, not a field deployment study. It holds the inherited TPR and FPR fixed while varying assumed attack prevalence. Projected PPV, NPV, alerts, analyst-hours, capacity comparisons, and relative-cost decisions therefore depend on the assumption that class-conditional operating behavior remains stable as prevalence changes. The projections do not model simultaneous covariate, protocol, topology, user, attacker, or extractor shift.

The traffic and workload quantities are likewise scenarios: one million benign flows per day, two minutes of analyst service per alert, declared analyst capacity, and relative costs with `C_FP:C_FN = 1:100`. These assumptions expose how precision, yield, alert volume, and workload can diverge, but they are not organization-specific observations. High projected PPV cannot be interpreted as realized SOC utility, and low projected workload can reflect negligible detection yield. The study therefore does not establish production alert burden, analyst performance, monetary return, or a universal operational preference.

Stage26 introduces a separate systems boundary. Measurements begin with prepared model input and end with materialized probabilities. They exclude packet capture, flow extraction, feature construction, representation conversion, alert aggregation, network transport, and analyst response. Batch size, software, hardware, backend, and resource conditions are fixed to the measured paths. Unsupported, timed-out, or resource-limited paths remain statuses rather than poor timing results. The observed CPU/GPU ratios thus support component-level feasibility only; they do not establish portable runtime constants, end-to-end IDS latency, energy behavior, or deployed system feasibility.

## 7.5 Computational and Representation Validity

The representation branches do not share one estimand. The early reference uses 78 numeric predictors; later temporal work uses a frozen ordered 70-feature representation; transfer uses bridge62 and bridge70 contracts; other branches use graph, temporal-window, packet-image, CNN, or ViT representations. Results from these branches cannot be pooled into an architecture ranking or a common deployment claim.

Stage18 graph evidence is source-restricted and cannot estimate unseen topology. The Stage18 tabular ViT branch was a feasibility rejection, not a failed performance comparison. Stage19 MTemporal and SingleScale results use strict temporal-window constructions that are not directly interchangeable with per-flow tree experiments. Stage21's CNN/ViT comparison is descriptive and based on a single frozen comparison. Integrated Gradients and attention results are bounded to documented neural contexts. These restrictions preserve construct validity by preventing unlike representations and measures from being treated as one model comparison, but they limit conclusions about architecture superiority and portability.

The main deployment results also inherit incomplete backend coverage. A missing or unsupported LightGBM GPU path cannot be interpreted as slower than a measured path, and a compatible path on a Tesla T4 or recorded CPU cannot be generalized to other hardware. Additional environments, batches, extraction implementations, and end-to-end pipelines would be required to resolve those uncertainties.

## 7.6 Reproducibility and Historical Artifact Availability

The repository provides a substantial provenance surface: protocol configurations, safe verification interfaces, archived executed notebooks, extracted or archival Python implementations, source maps, result manifests, hashes, equivalence records, and manuscript-critical traceability. These resources support review of the claim-to-artifact chain. They do not imply that every historical result can be regenerated locally.

Reproducibility status is heterogeneous. Stage25 supports deterministic scalar reproduction from inherited frozen operating-point inputs. Stage26 preserves the measurement methodology and archival hardware sessions, but its historical timings remain environment-specific. The other stages are generally partial or static evidence: safe interfaces are verify-only, and Stage28 is the final empirical wall. The audit reports 112 of 112 equivalence rows passing and 127 of 127 static tests at its accepted checkpoint, but these checks verify documented claims and artifacts rather than guaranteeing complete scientific reruns.

The original IDS2018 and CICIDS2017 source data, several processed corpora, historical models, probability or explanation arrays, and parts of the historical software environments are external, unavailable, hash-only, or intentionally closed. Closed targets and holdouts are not reopened by the safe interfaces. A notebook or source script therefore documents method history without guaranteeing bit-for-bit regeneration, while a hash verifies artifact identity without reproducing its scientific output. These are evidence-availability and reproducibility threats, not grounds for replacing missing values with estimates.

The manuscript should consequently distinguish available code from executed historical evidence, deterministic scalar checks from full model reruns, and provenance verification from source-data access. The limitation is explicit rather than hidden: the study supports claim-level and artifact-level verification, with exact deterministic reproduction where classified, but not complete end-to-end regeneration of all stages, models, explanations, timings, and target results.

## 7.7 Summary of Interpretation Boundaries

The controls and governance procedures materially strengthen the evidence: frozen memberships, training-only preprocessing, validation-only thresholds, source-only transfer, eligibility gates, registered seeds, target-opening records, cancellation receipts, and explicit unsupported statuses reduce several avoidable threats. They do not transform benchmark populations into live traffic, make K79 a session-level independence guarantee, make five seeds a population sample, convert analytic projections into field observations, or make unavailable artifacts reproducible.

The strongest defensible conclusions are therefore conditional: selected tree ensembles discriminate well on the processed reference; chronological development ranked higher on the evaluated shared target under five fixed realizations; transfer was directional; eligible-family behavior was selective; and measured inference paths were computationally feasible within their declared boundaries. Claims of arbitrary dataset portability, universal zero-day detection, causal explanation of the temporal contrast, end-to-end deployment performance, universal architecture superiority, and complete local reproduction remain unjustified.
