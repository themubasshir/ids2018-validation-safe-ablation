## Bounded fit assessment

The manuscript is positioned as a bounded, source-faithful empirical validity audit of intrusion-detection evaluation. It examines protocol geometry, temporal and cross-dataset transfer, operating-point interpretation under prevalence assumptions, component-level computation, shortcut sensitivity, and eligible leave-one-attack-family-out behavior. It does not present a routine application of an existing model on a standard security dataset as the sole contribution. The contribution is the evidence-governed audit framing and multi-axis empirical validity analysis, with explicit limits on population, chronology, thresholds, hardware, family eligibility, and source availability.

This positioning may be relevant to JISA's information-security application scope, but editorial fit is not established. No novelty claim has been enlarged, and no exclusion has been inferred from inaccessible or third-party summaries.
