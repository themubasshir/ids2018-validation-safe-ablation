# Phase 9C.3 author and publisher disclosure checklist

## Elsevier generative-AI policy

Official policy reviewed: https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals

Policy page states that authors remain responsible for accuracy, comprehensiveness, impartiality, originality, source checking, privacy, and rights. Generative-AI use in manuscript preparation requires a separate disclosure naming the tool/service, purpose, and author oversight. AI used in research/code workflows should be described reproducibly in Methods. AI tools cannot be authors. AI-generated research figures/data must not fabricate or alter results; permitted data visualizations must derive reproducibly from underlying data. Policy page reports update June 2026.
