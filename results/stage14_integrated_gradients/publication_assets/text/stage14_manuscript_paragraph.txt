All 64 Integrated Gradients explanations satisfied numerical completeness, but reference robustness varied substantially. Only 10 cases (15.6%) satisfied every conservative study-specific robustness criterion; 29 (45.3%) were moderately reference-stable and 25 (39.1%) were reference-sensitive. No false-negative explanation qualified as reference-robust. The MLP and CNN global absolute-importance profiles were strongly aligned (cosine 0.911), although their top-10 feature sets overlapped only moderately (Jaccard 0.429) and their signed-attribution agreement was lower (cosine 0.791). We therefore emphasize aggregate recurring features and restrict main-text case examples to explanations that pass the explicit reference-robustness audit.
