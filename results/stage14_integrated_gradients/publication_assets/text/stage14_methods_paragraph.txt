Integrated Gradients was applied to the pre-sigmoid attack logit of the archived MLP and one-dimensional CNN models. Inputs were standardized with the original training scaler. To reduce reliance on a single arbitrary reference, attributions were averaged across 32 deterministic benign training records. A convergence audit compared 16, 32, 64, and 128 trapezoidal integration steps. The 128-step configuration was retained because it was the lowest audited resolution that achieved complete cross-model compliance with the prespecified completeness and attribution-convergence criteria. The final outcome-stratified panel contained eight true positives, true negatives, false positives, and false negatives for each architecture.
