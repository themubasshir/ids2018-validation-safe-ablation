# Prohibited final claims

These formulations exceed the frozen Stage1–28 evidence ceiling and must not appear as conclusions of the final paper:

1. “The model generalizes.”
2. “The model detects zero-day attacks.”
3. “Temporal splitting always causes catastrophic collapse.”
4. “Shortcut features cause all transfer failure.”
5. “Deep learning is universally inferior.”
6. “ViT performed poorly in Stage18.” No Stage18 ViT model was executed.
7. “The model is operationally useless in all real SOCs.”
8. “The IDS literature is invalid.”
9. “This proves supervised IDS cannot detect novel attacks.”
10. “Graph context causally improves intrusion detection.”
11. “The frozen 5% validation FPR constraint transfers to deployment.”
12. “Stage25 projections are observed field performance.”
13. “Stage26 timings are hardware-independent deployment constants.”
14. “The Stage24 forward and reverse directions form one average transfer score.”
15. “The Stage27 native metrics form one aggregate zero-day score.”

Allowed replacements are scope-bound: “under the evaluated conditions,” “for the eligible families,” “under the frozen prior-shift assumption,” “on the measured hardware,” and “the results indicate.” Cancelled cells never support claims, descriptive 5/5 orderings are not significance tests, and conditional intervals remain conditional.
