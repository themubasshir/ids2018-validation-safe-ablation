# Stage29 master figure plan

Stage29 inventories 105 unique figure families represented by 185 existing files. No figure was regenerated, edited, or scientifically recomputed. The registry evaluates the canonical family once while recording duplicated formats and publication-package copies.

## Proposed main exhibits

The nine MAIN candidate families should become six conceptual exhibits during a separately authorized manuscript-assembly phase:

1. **Temporal validity (combined two-panel exhibit).** Combine `fig22r_1_validation_to_final_pr_auc` and `fig22r_2_validation_to_final_roc_auc`. Supports CLM29-008 and retains PR-AUC and ROC-AUC as separate panels rather than a common score.
2. **Shortcut/split interaction.** Retain `figure_23_a_subset_split_interaction`. Supports CLM29-010 and must be accompanied by the placebo/causal limitation.
3. **Cross-dataset directionality (combined two-panel exhibit).** Combine `fig24_1_normalized_pr_auc_directionality` and `fig24_2_roc_auc_directionality`. Supports CLM29-011; forward and reverse directions remain separate.
4. **Prevalence stress.** Retain `figure25_a_ppv_cliff`. Supports CLM29-013 under the frozen prior-shift assumption.
5. **Deployment profiling.** Retain `F26_CPU1_GPU_P95_SPEEDUP`. Supports CLM29-014 only on the measured Stage26 hardware/software; component-level timing is not complete IDS latency.
6. **Unseen-family validity (combined two-panel exhibit).** Combine `stage27_primary_roc_auc_ci` and `stage27_balanced_recall_ci`. Supports CLM29-015 while preserving ranking and operating-point behavior as separate panels and marking Infiltration descriptive.

Stage28 supplies stability tables rather than a new figure. Its final evidence should annotate the temporal and LOAO narrative, not be turned into a newly computed plot in Stage29.

## Supplement policy

Forty-one candidate families remain supplementary. They preserve uncertainty, calibration, explanation reliability, graph/architecture chronology, threshold transfer, control analyses, detailed prevalence projections, and the wider deployment profile. The 55 excluded families remain in the repository but should not appear in the paper because they are individual-case, historical, peripheral, or redundant with higher-information assets.

`COMBINE_LATER` is an architecture instruction, not authorization to edit graphics. Scientific values, axes, and native metrics must remain unchanged. Any later combination requires explicit manuscript-rewrite approval and visual verification.
