# Discussion structure

1. **Benchmark performance versus deployment evidence.** Interpret CLM29-001 alongside the temporal, cross-dataset, prevalence, and family counterevidence; avoid calling benchmark metrics invalid.
2. **Ranking versus operating-point transfer.** Connect the Stage18 graph counterexample, Stage22 threshold failure, Stage24 transfer, and Stage27 LOAO behavior without averaging native metrics.
3. **Shortcut learning and explanation validity.** Discuss stable-but-fidelity-limited LIME, conditional feature-subset interactions, placebo evidence, and why causality is unresolved (CLM29-004; CLM29-010).
4. **Temporal validity.** Explain five-seed stability of the direction while retaining chronology/family entanglement, reopened-historical-holdout status, and mixed controls (CLM29-008; CLM29-016).
5. **Cross-dataset limitations.** Emphasize direction, bridge restriction, serialization sensitivity, different prevalence, and cancellation rather than one portability score (CLM29-011; CLM29-012).
6. **Base-rate effects and SOC interpretation.** Separate PPV, capacity, alert volume, detection yield, and relative cost; projections are conditional decision aids, not field validation (CLM29-013).
7. **Architecture complexity and practical utility.** Discuss representation validity, descriptive Stage21 architecture results, component-level profiling, and hardware specificity (CLM29-006; CLM29-007; CLM29-014).
8. **Novelty and family generalization.** Explain selective family transfer, learner dependence, ineligibility, low support, and ranking/threshold divergence without “zero-day detection” language (CLM29-015).
9. **Model and seed dependence.** State what the five frozen seeds resolve and what finite realization scope/full-HPO limits leave open.
10. **Implications for IDS evaluation practice.** Recommend separated validity axes, target locks, direction-specific transfers, prevalence-aware reporting, hardware receipts, eligibility/support gates, and claim-to-artifact provenance.
11. **Limitations.** Surface LIM29-001–LIM29-018 in the main paper; supplementary detail does not replace main disclosure.
12. **Future work, explicitly non-evidentiary.** Prospective live-network validation, independent blind datasets, richer family taxonomies, calibrated deployment adaptation, additional hardware, and leakage-safe temporal representations. Do not introduce GNN/federated-learning work as evidence for this paper.
