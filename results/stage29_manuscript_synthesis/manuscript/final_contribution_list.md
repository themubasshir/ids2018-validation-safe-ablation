# Final contribution list

The recommended paper has six defensible contributions:

1. **A connected, validation-safe IDS evaluation framework.** The work treats benchmark/reference validity, split/temporal validity, shortcut sensitivity, cross-dataset validity, prevalence/operational validity, computational validity, unseen-family validity, and realization stability as distinct axes reported in their native metrics (E29-006; E29-020–E29-041).
2. **A source-faithful temporal and operating-point audit with stability closure.** On one shared forward target, chronological development produced higher ranking than random development across five frozen seeds, while frozen thresholds transferred poorly and controls did not isolate a sole causal mechanism (E29-016; E29-020; E29-021; E29-037; E29-038; E29-040).
3. **A bounded audit of shortcut sensitivity and cross-dataset transportability.** Feature-removal and control effects were split- and learner-dependent, while bidirectional IDS2018/CICIDS2017 transfer was strongly asymmetric and two unrecoverable cells remained cancelled (E29-022–E29-027).
4. **An assumption-explicit operational and computational stress test.** Frozen operating points were translated across prevalence, workload, and cost scenarios, then paired with hardware-specific deployment profiles and explicit unsupported runtime paths (E29-012; E29-028–E29-033).
5. **An eligible-family novelty audit that preserves learner dependence and low support.** Several families retained stable behavior, BOT remained learner-dependent, two families were ineligible, and Infiltration remained descriptive at support 36 (E29-034–E29-036; E29-039).
6. **A claim-to-provenance reproducibility layer.** Manuscript claims and numbers resolve through frozen artifacts, protocols, canonical code, archived notebooks, equivalence evidence, and disclosed reproducibility gaps (E29-001–E29-041; accepted repository-wide reproducibility registries).

These contributions do not assert universal model generalization, zero-day detection, a causal shortcut or drift mechanism, field-validated SOC utility, hardware-independent timing, or complete end-to-end rerun reproducibility.
