# Methods structure

The final Methods should be organized by scientific components, not Stage1–Stage28 chronology.

## 1. Datasets, labels, and provenance

- Processed CSE-CIC-IDS2018 reference population and frozen memberships (Stages1–5).
- Source-faithful IDS2018 temporal populations (Stages18, 20, 22).
- CICIDS2017 source/target contracts and semantic bridges (Stage24).
- Attack-family taxonomy, eligibility, and support (Stages11, 27).
- Artifact/config/notebook/equivalence chain and execution boundaries (repository consolidation; Stage29 synthesis only).

## 2. Reference models and operating points

- Baseline inventory, validation-only tuning, balanced/security threshold rules, and closed holdout evaluation (Stages2–5).
- Global/local explanation diagnostics and uncertainty/calibration/cost context (Stages6, 8–14).
- Treat models as reference instruments, not a proposed architecture contribution.

## 3. Split, duplicate, and temporal validity

- Membership/duplicate sanitization and shared final-target geometry (Stages1, 22).
- Timestamp feasibility and why inherited windows were rejected (Stage18).
- Frozen random/chronological × natural/rebalanced design and no post-holdout adaptation (Stage22).
- Five-seed linkage and family-aware controls (Stage28).

## 4. Shortcut-feature audit

- Seven primary feature subsets, matched-size placebos, stumps, proxy-absorption, and family composition (Stage23).
- Explain why interactions are sensitivity evidence rather than causal identification.

## 5. Cross-dataset protocol

- Direction-specific source/target roles, bridge62/bridge70 contracts, no target-guided selection, flag serialization audit, and GROUNDED_S4 cancellation (Stage24).

## 6. Prevalence and operational stress

- Prior-probability-shift equations already frozen in the repository, prevalence grid, PPV/NPV, alerts, workload, capacity, and relative-cost scenarios (Stages10, 25).
- Make analyst time and cost assumptions explicit.

## 7. Deployment profiling

- Eligible artifacts, component timing boundary, warm/cold and batch protocols, CPU/GPU matching, memory/artifact size, and unsupported statuses (Stage26).
- Record hardware/software without implying universal timings.

## 8. Unseen-family protocol

- Day-atomic TRAIN < VALIDATION < TARGET construction, eligibility gates, ranking versus frozen threshold metrics, support rules, and descriptive similarity analysis (Stage27).
- Five-seed and random-LOAO controls (Stage28).

## 9. Uncertainty, locking, and anti-adaptation

- Historical paired bootstrap procedures are reported as frozen conditional evidence (Stages8–10, 23–24).
- Training-seed variation remains distinct from sampling uncertainty (Stages12, 28).
- No post-target model, threshold, feature, or calibration adaptation; cancelled cells remain cancelled.

## 10. Reproducibility and provenance

- Map every manuscript number/claim to artifact, config, canonical code, archived notebook, equivalence receipt, and disclosed limitation.
- State which evidence is scalar-reproducible versus partially reproducible; do not claim complete full reruns.
