# Results structure

## 1. Conventional benchmark success establishes the reference

Report the compact benchmark table and CLM29-001–CLM29-003. Show that the reference result is strong while balanced learner and calibration winners remain unresolved. Do not open with all baseline candidates.

## 2. Chronology changes ranking evidence and exposes threshold transfer

Lead with Stage28 five-seed closure for CLM29-008, then show CLM29-009. Use the combined temporal PR/ROC exhibit and temporal five-seed table. Close with CLM29-016: controls do not isolate a sole causal mechanism.

## 3. Shortcut sensitivity is conditional, not a single leakage mechanism

Present CLM29-010 with the subset/split interaction figure. Keep placebos, stumps, family composition, and SHAP proxy details in supplement while naming the causal limit in main text.

## 4. Cross-dataset transfer is directional

Report forward and reverse PR-AUC/ROC-AUC separately for CLM29-011. Use the two-panel directionality exhibit and bidirectional table. Keep conditional flag sensitivity supplementary and state the GROUNDED_S4 cancellation (CLM29-012) visibly.

## 5. Base rates separate discrimination, PPV, workload, and yield

Use CLM29-013, the PPV-cliff figure, and the standard 0.1% operational table. Preserve prior-shift, analyst-time, and cost assumptions next to results.

## 6. Computational feasibility is hardware and component specific

Use one CPU/GPU exhibit for CLM29-014. State component-level boundary and unsupported paths. Move full timing, memory, cold-start, Pareto, and backend tables to supplement.

## 7. Unseen-family behavior is selective and learner dependent

Use CLM29-015, the paired LOAO ranking/recall exhibit, and Stage28 stability table. Name ineligible families and keep Infiltration descriptive. Do not use an aggregate zero-day score.

## 8. Integrated validity matrix

Close Results with `validity_axis_matrix.csv`: what changed, survived, failed, and remains unknown in each native metric. This is a framework synthesis, not a new experimental result.
