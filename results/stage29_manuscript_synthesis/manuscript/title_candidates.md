# Title candidates

Titles are ranked within and across three styles. None claims universal failure or deployment proof.

## A. Conservative academic

1. **A Multi-Axis Validation Audit of Machine-Learning Intrusion Detection** — Most direct and accurately broad; recommended overall rank 2.
2. **Evaluating the Robustness of Benchmark Intrusion Detection Across Temporal, Dataset, and Deployment Conditions** — Specific but longer; overall rank 5.
3. **From Benchmark Evaluation to Deployment Stress Testing in Machine-Learning Intrusion Detection** — Clear progression; overall rank 6.
4. **An Empirical Audit of Validation Validity in Benchmark Intrusion Detection** — Compact but repeats “validation validity”; overall rank 10.

## B. Validation-framework centered

1. **Beyond Benchmark Scores: A Validation Framework for Intrusion Detection** — Concise, accurate, and memorable without overclaim; recommended overall rank 1.
2. **A Validation Chain for Intrusion Detection: Time, Transfer, Prevalence, Deployment, and Novelty** — Makes the connected axes explicit; overall rank 3.
3. **Validation-Safe Evaluation of Intrusion Detection Under Distribution and Deployment Stress** — Highlights protocol discipline; overall rank 4.
4. **Auditing Intrusion Detection Across the Benchmark-to-Deployment Validation Chain** — Strong organizing metaphor; overall rank 7.
5. **Evidence-Bounded Validation of Machine-Learning Intrusion Detection** — Emphasizes claim discipline; overall rank 8.

## C. Memorable but defensible

1. **What Survives the Benchmark? A Multi-Axis Audit of Intrusion Detection** — Engaging and faithful to the conclusion question; overall rank 9.
2. **When High Benchmark Scores Travel Poorly: Auditing Intrusion Detection Validity** — Directionally useful but “poorly” may overemphasize negatives; overall rank 11.
3. **Ranking Is Not a Threshold: Stress-Testing Intrusion Detection Beyond the Benchmark** — Captures a recurring counterexample but is narrower than the whole paper; overall rank 12.
4. **Strong on the Benchmark, Conditional Beyond It: An Intrusion Detection Audit** — Accurate but slightly rhetorical; overall rank 13.

Do not choose a final title until the integrated manuscript and target journal are approved.
